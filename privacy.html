PRIVACY POLICY

Website privacy policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

BubblesTheDev respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit this website and how that information is handled. It applies only to this website, not to any BubblesTheDev Android application.

Information collected by this website

This is a static informational website. BubblesTheDev does not use this website to directly collect personal information. The site has no account registration, sign-in, contact forms, comments, purchases, or user-submitted content.

The website does not include first-party analytics, advertising trackers, tracking pixels, or technologies that store information in your browser such as cookies, local storage, or session storage.

GitHub Pages hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may automatically process technical information needed to deliver and secure the service. GitHub's Pages documentation states that a visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. GitHub may also process information such as browser or device details, referring pages, requested pages, and the date and time of a request.

GitHub handles this information under its own General Privacy Statement . BubblesTheDev does not receive or maintain a separate database of this hosting information.

Third-party links

This website links to third-party services, including Google Play. If you follow one of these links, the third party may collect information according to its own privacy policy. BubblesTheDev does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites and services.

Children's privacy

This website is intended to provide general information about software projects and is not designed to collect personal information from children. BubblesTheDev does not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Data retention and your choices

Because BubblesTheDev does not directly collect personal information through this website, there is no website visitor database maintained by BubblesTheDev to access, correct, or delete. Requests concerning information processed by GitHub should be directed to GitHub using the options described in its privacy statement.

You may limit information shared automatically with websites by using privacy features in your browser or device. Blocking essential network information may prevent the site from loading correctly.

Android app privacy

Each BubblesTheDev application may have different features and data practices. This website policy does not replace an app-specific privacy policy. Review the privacy information provided on the relevant Google Play listing before using an application.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the website's features, hosting, or legal requirements change. Any revision will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or an application, use the developer contact information shown on the relevant BubblesTheDev Google Play listing.
